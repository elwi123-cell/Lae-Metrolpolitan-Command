Command profile

Policing Lae City

Lae Metropolitan Command operates within the Royal Papua New Guinea Constabulary's Northern Command structure.

Metropolitan focus

Lae Metropolitan Command is responsible for policing Lae City, while Morobe Provincial Police Command has province-wide responsibilities.

Four-station delivery

RPNGC planning material describes policing services in the metropolitan area as being delivered through police stations across the command.

Community partnership

Effective policing depends on cooperation between police, communities, government agencies, businesses and other stakeholders.

Professional service

Our public-facing information is designed to support lawful, impartial and professional policing and public confidence.
